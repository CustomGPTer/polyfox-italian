The first version of PolyFox Italian: a free Italian course for Android, from the first word to level C2. No adverts, no account, no internet.

- 180 units from A1 to C2: 2,160 words and about 2,560 sentences, all spoken, with 540 grammar points and 359 grammar rules.
- Standard Italian. Lessons start with tu; Lei, the polite you, comes in at A2.
- Speaking is checked on the phone by a speech model inside the app. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
- Twelve conversations, forty-two stories, verb tables for 402 verbs in eleven tenses, a grammar book and an exam for each level.
- Choose whether lessons teach the man's or the woman's form ("sono stanco" or "sono stanca"). Both are always accepted.
- Wrong answers show which words were wrong and why.
- Installs beside PolyFox French, not over it. Android 8 or later.
