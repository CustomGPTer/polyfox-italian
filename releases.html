Release notes

Install a new version over the old one. Your progress, streak and settings are kept.

1.0.0 26 November 2026

The first version of PolyFox Italian: the whole PolyFox course, written for Italian from the first word to level C2.

  • 180 units from A1 to C2: 2,160 words and about 2,560 sentences, all spoken, with 540 grammar points and 359 grammar rules.
  • Standard Italian. Lessons start with tu; Lei, the polite you, comes in at A2.
  • Speaking is checked on the phone by a speech model inside the app. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
  • Twelve conversations, from ordering at the bar to a job interview. Say or type your part, or tap Help.
  • Forty-two stories, read aloud, with questions at the end.
  • Verb tables for 402 verbs in eleven tenses, including the passato remoto, showing whether each verb takes avere or essere.
  • Choose whether lessons teach the man's or the woman's form of lines like "sono stanco" and "sono stanca". Both are always accepted.
  • Wrong answers show which words were wrong and why: gender, il, lo and la, elision, non, agreement, verb endings, è and e.
  • Accents are forgiven up to A2 and count from B1.
  • A new icon: the fox in front of the Italian flag. PolyFox Italian installs beside PolyFox French, not over it.
